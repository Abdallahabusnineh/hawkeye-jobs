# Country names

`countries.json` is derived from [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries)
(commit c2ac0049c14edcf2436c7aa1b2493222a020b462) by `tools/update_countries.py`: the UN member
states plus Palestine, Hong Kong and Macau, with their common English and Arabic names and a few
corrections.
